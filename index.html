How I work with spatial data

How I carry a project from raw spatial data to results people can use.

  1. 01

    Integrate

    Bring heterogeneous spatial data together: harmonise coordinate systems, check attributes and keys, keep every record traceable.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Automated validation checks and unit tests, so data problems surface early and every run can be repeated.

  3. 03

    Analyse and model

    Spatial joins, aggregation and raster preparation; deep-learning models trained, adapted and applied to aerial imagery and LiDAR-derived data.

  4. 04

    Evaluate

    Compare results with reference data, quantify errors and inspect where in space they occur — and state what the evidence does not show.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Results people can use: GIS projects for QGIS and ArcGIS Pro, notebooks, reports, presentations and interactive web pages.
