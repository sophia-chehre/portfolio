Applied research project · 10/2025 – 01/2026

Research and Concept Development – Circular Rental Model

Business Pilot Project, Catalyst Centre (co-funded by the European Union) · led by the Collaborating Centre on Sustainable Consumption and Production (CSCP) with KREFELD BUSINESS

A four-month business pilot project to design an incentive-based circular rental model and its implementation roadmap for the future Startup Centre Krefeld, housed in a former silk factory. I worked in an interdisciplinary team with businesses, public stakeholders and mentors. Certificate for active and outstanding participation.

My contribution

  • Researched and analysed good practices for circular rental and tenant incentives.
  • Co-developed tenant commitments and incentive mechanisms that link circular behaviour to concrete benefits such as fee reductions, visibility and shared services.
  • Supported stakeholder coordination and qualitative interviews used to validate the concept.
  • Co-produced the Circular Rental Agreement prototype and its implementation roadmap.
